ABOUT THIS WORKSPACE

A focused prototype, built for human review.

Sentinel currently analyzes usernames you enter manually. It does not monitor social media, messaging services, or app stores, and it does not verify whether a profile is authentic. Use authorized sources and investigate every potential match before drawing conclusions.
