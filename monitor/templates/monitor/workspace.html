SECURITY AND SCOPE

Operational limitations

  • No social-network scraping, authentication bypass, or background monitoring.
  • CSV input is limited to UTF-8 CSV up to 1 MB and 100 candidate records per submission.
  • Image/logo similarity is not implemented; image references are not collected or analyzed.
  • Risk scores are deterministic heuristic screening signals, not proof of impersonation.
  • Reviewer authentication/authorization and production-grade secret management must be configured before deployment.
